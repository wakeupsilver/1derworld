New apps show up on their own: drop a folder with an index.html into ~/Documents/projects or ~/Developer, or add it to projects.json, then reload.

How to 1derworld

  • WS/↑↓ walk · ←→ turn · AD sidestep · Shift run · Space jump
  • Drag to look around (like the Scratch bedroom) · scroll wheel steps forward and back
  • Click anything glowing under the cursor to use it (or E)
  • Walk into a portal to launch that app. Esc or the ⟵ button comes back.
  • 1–7 warp between zones · Tab portal list · L Constructive Lens · M mute
  • Trampolines stack height: keep bouncing, hold Space on landing. Land on the mezzanine, take the slide.
  • Red-rimmed hatches are trapdoors. They go somewhere good.
  • Every buffet food is a key. Plug in a MIDI keyboard and the world plays along.
